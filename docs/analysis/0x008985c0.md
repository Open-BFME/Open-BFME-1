temporary lifetimes needed for the frame and cleanup sharing. Their float
calls must be checked against x87: `_CIfmod` and `__ftol2` take operands in
x87 registers, not an ordinary stack-passed double. The earlier bank also
inverted the undefined guard, omitted type4, and used the wrong type29 ABI.
Those are bank defects; they are not all attributed to Ghidra.

Specific neighbour corrections are actionable:

- **008B6880:** weekday/month entries are inline four-byte character arrays,
