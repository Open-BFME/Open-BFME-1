## Sorting call ABI

At renderer +0x0EDA..+0x0EF3, retail passes the address of three zeroed floats
at EBP-0x70/-0x6C/-0x68, followed by four unsigned-short values in dword stack
slots. The call is independently decoded to RVA 0x0093B340; caller cleanup is
0x14 bytes. The separately matched 61-byte wrapper at RVA 0x009037D0 passes the
same 12-byte local. Its legacy source calls this a SphereClass, but canonical
SphereClass is 16 bytes. Do not reproduce that misleading type declaration.

The full 1,257-byte callee at 0x0093B340 has no incoming this register. With
sorting disabled it forwards arguments 2..5 to independently matched
DX8Wrapper::Draw_Triangles (0x00906DF0). With sorting enabled it reads the first
argument pointer at +0x3A6; only offsets +8 at +0x3D6, +4 at +0x40F, and +0 at
+0x44A are consumed as floating coordinates in the matrix/depth calculation.
No fourth float is accessed or copied. Its exits at +0x27, +0x4C5, +0x4DC,
and +0x4E8 are plain RET, and the callers ignore a result. The correct bounded
view is a cdecl void function taking a reference to an opaque 12-byte center
record plus four unsigned shorts. The bank uses `Rva0093B340Center` and
`rva0093B340`, preserving the address rather than guessing the changed semantic
signature. A future landing needs one independently justified pin for
`?rva0093B340@@YAXABURva0093B340Center@@GGGG@Z` at 0x0093B340; the pre-pin
consistency query found no existing pin. This evidence-only commit adds no pin.

## Measurement and exhausted levers

The bank compiles to 4,100 bytes with 149 aligned relocation sites and 35
non-relocation byte differences, all in +0x063A..+0x065F. Its positional score
is (4100-35)/4100 = 0.9914634146341463; this is not a byte-match claim. The
first two in-place matrix rows, all later code, frame allocation, calls and
branch offsets agree. The last row names its Z product before the sum; the
canonical Matrix3D::Transform_Vector call instead has 248 byte differences,
a two-byte x87-length change, and resulting alignment drift through +0x7AE.

Retail consumes Y with FXCH/FMUL, adds the Z product, then consumes X and
adds translation. VC7.1 keeps more original inputs live and pops them later.
Tested: six flat term orders; named XY/XZ/YZ partials and individual products;
float/double partials; input/output copies, scalar/array/aggregate aliases;
conditional alias-copy variants; destructive scratch products; legitimate
loop/local scope and declaration-order changes; /Op, /Op-, /Oa, /Ow, /G5 and
/G7; isolated TU versus original sibling TU. Diagnostic unused-local counts
1..12 were all unchanged and are not in the bank. Mechanical EH choices were
also run before the manual pass. No source, flags or pin makes this exact yet.
No nonmatching body is left under game/, and the original lift remains intact.
