Independent identity evidence supplied by fresh_lifts analyst and independently
checked against the retail call/constructor instructions:

  VA 0x01085EC0 from RVA 0x0012E63D. Its data factory is VA 0x00408968,

  invokes ILT 0x0001659F, and retains the resulting vtable 0x010A4210.
- ILT 0x0001659F jumps directly to RVA 0x001FCEC0. This constructor installs
  vtable 0x010A4210, constructs a narrow string at +0x14, four owning
  references at +0x18 through +0x24, and two narrow-string vectors at

  and Closed, and copies the shipped DWORD at VA 0x012ADC38 into +0x40/+0x44.

  from RVA 0x0012E67A through dataProc VA 0x004043F4 -> RVA 0x00123BF0.
  It calls the same base constructor and then overwrites the vptr with

  calls ILT 0x0001659F and performs that derived-vptr overwrite.

Therefore the existing GateOpenAndCloseBehaviorModuleData constructor name is
retained. Old comments claiming identical-code folding or a GateProxy identity
for the base constructor are not evidence. The constructor conversion is
banked pending the complete virtual contract; this publication candidate
contains only the four-byte datum. It does not change constructor or destructor
ledger identities.

The existing 483-byte extent ends at exclusive RVA 0x001FD0A3. The source
probe reproduces all 483 bytes modulo 24 relocation slots. Scoped strict
verification checks the relocation targets separately; a probe alone is not
