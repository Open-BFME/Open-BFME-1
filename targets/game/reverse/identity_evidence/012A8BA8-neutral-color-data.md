# Neutral player color at VA 0x012A8BA8

The datum had no ledger owner. This change retains the existing descriptive spelling `?g_neutralColor000DA610@@3IA` and its ordinary unsigned-int declaration. It defines four initialized storage bytes in PlayerInit.cpp, records the data row, and admits its DIR32 spelling. It introduces no alias or new consumer.

Retail SHA-256 is 1fbdc0ced8f283732c094c4f0804ce8dc1e3e3675b720bcab066c94fed964e75. VA 0x012A8BA8 (RVA 0x00EA8BA8) is backed by initialized .data and holds `40 40 40 FF`, the 32-bit packed color 0xFF404040. A whole-image byte search for every VA in the surrounding 33-byte interval finds exactly one reference, the absolute dword load at VA 0x004DAB72 (`A1 A8 8B 2A 01`). There are no references into the remaining bytes, indexed accesses, address escapes, or data pointers to this storage in that search. No data row overlaps its four-byte range, and no DIR32, pin, or export name starts inside it. The value is read from writable storage rather than emitted as a compiler immediate.

The receiver is Player::init's live ESI Player pointer. No additional argument is consumed by this load. In the no-template branch, retail stores the loaded EAX into Player+0x1C4 and Player+0x1C8 at VA 0x004DAB87 and VA 0x004DAB8D. This is an opaque packed color word; no signed arithmetic or pointer use occurs. Its unsigned-int representation preserves every bit including the high alpha byte. Zero Hour Common/RTS/Player.cpp assigns NEUTRAL_PLAYER_COLOR to m_color and m_nightColor in the corresponding no-template branch. Zero Hour's macro is white (0xFFFFFFFF), whereas BFME's verified stored value is dark gray. This is contextual identity evidence, not a claim that BFME retained that constant or Zero Hour's signed Color typedef.
