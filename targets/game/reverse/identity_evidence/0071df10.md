# Shrub vertex/index loader at 0x0071DF10

The native boundary is `[0x0071DF10, 0x0071E7CF)`: 2,239 bytes,
with `ret 4` at +0x8BC followed by INT3 padding. The matched 4,131-byte
`W3DShrubBuffer::rva0071EEC0` renderer calls this body through ILT 0x0000E282.
Its buffer arrays +0x04/+0x54, 12,000 records of size 0xA4 at +0x1548,
type table +0x1E1CD4, and flags +0x1E1CCC/+0x1E1CCF independently agree with
the shrub constructor and matched update/append/render siblings. Keep the
method address-derived: these witnesses prove the owner and role, not an
original method spelling. The unused incoming pointer agrees with the
already-published `rva0071DF10(void *)` pin.

## Corrected bank, 2026-09-26

The old preferred bank emitted 2,061 bytes, with 1,873 differing non-relocation
bytes and 23 displaced relocation sites. Its `score=0.82` was an author
estimate, not measured positional byte agreement. It contained behavior and
source-structure errors:

- The Zero Hour index-loop try/catch is absent from this retail body. Removing
  it restores `push ebp; mov ebp,esp; and esp,-8` before EH registration. The
  actual two eight-byte buffer write-lock objects remain inside the loop.
- Retail transforms normals with the complete `Matrix3D::Transform_Vector`,
  including its alias check and translation terms. The bank used only a 3x3
  calculation plus a redundant null guard.
- Vertex input with its type offset and transformed output are separate
  vectors. The in-place approximation lost a twelve-byte temporary and changed
  the floating-point evaluation. Separate vectors recover the 0xD4 frame.
- Retail clears the sway value when the type-data byte at +0x54 is set. The
  old source always emitted the record's sway value.
- Retail checks the UV/color buffers, but does not check the enclosing material
  descriptor. The new source follows those observed guards and reloads the
  mesh after the normals call. The UV/color accessors require force-inlining
  in this TU to reproduce retail's observed inline code.
- The normals call has a pointer return. The old union between incompatible
  void-return and pointer-return member functions has been removed. The
  lighting calls now use the independently matched protected `doLighting` ABI.

The corrected bank compiles to **2,240 bytes**, with **805** differing
non-relocation bytes and **six** displaced relocation sites. The first byte
difference at +0x32 is an early-out displacement to the one-byte-longer tail.
This is **not an exact match** and claims no progress. Its score is the
conservative positional agreement `(2239 - 805) / 2240 = 0.6401785714`,
penalizing the extra byte. It is deliberately not scored by instruction
similarity. The former preferred source is preserved in attempt history;
replacing its incomparable 0.82 estimate avoids serving the known-bad body.

The remaining residue is the vertex/index EDI/EBX mirror, the operand order
of a vertex-capacity LEA, x87 scheduling in the vertex and normal transforms,
and the movement/darkening calculation. The emitted darkening path also has
an extra intermediate store; the equivalent single-assignment spelling
changed register allocation throughout the body. Its exact source form
remains unresolved. The virtual +0x34 output is represented by Vector3
storage, but only its first float is consumed; that pointee identity is still
an explicit hypothesis, not an independently named callee claim.

## Callee contracts

`tools/callees.py 0x0071DF10 2239` identifies index/vertex write-lock constructors
and destructors, `VertexMaterialClass::Get_Emissive`, `WWMath::Inv_Sqrt` through
ILT 0x00027A43, shrub `doLighting` through ILT 0x0000E9FD, and 0x009272E0.
The matched `doLighting` body at 0x0071D280 takes normal, terrain lighting,
emissive, packed diffuse, and float scale, returning unsigned packed color.

The 44-byte body at 0x009272E0 saves ECX, reads its dirty flag at +0x18,
optionally invokes virtual +0x10, then returns the result of the independently
matched `MeshGeometryClass::get_vert_normals(bool)` at 0x00927230 in EAX and
executes `ret 4` at +0x29. Both the aligned shrub call and independent tree
loader call pass integer zero. The bank uses opaque
`Rva009272E0Mesh::rva009272e0(int)` returning `const Vector3 *`; it intentionally
does not reuse the existing incorrectly void-typed `BfmeC998::bfmeGo998C`.
No new symbol pin was added. A later exact landing must prove/check and add
that pointer-return dependency as a bounded repair.

## Rejected levers

This pass tested canonical native headers and buffer/material views; local
and field-definition order; buffer aliases; direct and cached GlobalData
reads; scalar versus vector virtual output storage; complete and in-place
transforms; XYZ/XY temporaries; vector `+=`, value addition and scaling;
movement-product order and caching; darkening-store order and temporaries;
and generated register/copy/store/loop choices. The generic shape search
found no improvement. Probe was rerun on the final formatted bank, and
`docs/shape_levers.md` was checked. No nonmatching game source, assembly,
shared header edits, ledger claims, or speculative pins were introduced.

The final bank retains the established `MaterialInfoClass`, `MeshClass`, and
`TriIndex` names for the witnessed BFME layout views. Re-probing after those
name-preserving edits gives the same 2240/2239 and 805-byte result. The final
immutable named-source archive is `848d78ecc8ad474cabf94c2e28f090be45df462ec3a2147ad623edb517625d4f.json`.
