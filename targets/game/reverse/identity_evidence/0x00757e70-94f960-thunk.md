The constructor at RVA 0x00757E70 calls RVA 0x0094F960 at offset +0x311. The 11-byte thunk adds 0xE0 to ECX then jumps to RVA 0x00960050. The matched body at 0x00960050 is `SegLineRendererClass::Set_Texture`, as `targets/game/reverse/functions.csv` and `targets/game/reverse/symbols.csv` record.
