The ledger claims 11543 bytes at RVA 00397540 under ?logicMessageDispatcher@GameLogic@@, with object-symbol _bfme_GameLogic_logicMessageDispatcher_397540. The source is a __declspec(naked) function containing exactly 11543 literal __emit bytes, individually equal to the retail prefix. Its metadata incorrectly calls this a C++ conversion from MASM.

The claim ends at 0039A257, inside the seven-byte instruction MOV FS:[0],ECX at 0039A251..0039A257. The omitted byte completes that instruction. Retail then executes POP EBX at 0039A258, ADD ESP,0B8h at 0039A259 and RET 8 at 0039A25F..0039A261. Thus the executable instruction stream is 11554 bytes, not 11543. All 3322 instructions decode exactly from the entry; no direct branch leaves that stream. Ghidra read_memory at VA 0079A240 independently agrees with the local PE tail.

Their witnessed bounds give 6,4,10,25,49 entries respectively; all 94 pointers target instructions inside the executable stream. The first two use 85-byte and 22-byte remap tables whose indices stay within those bounds. These tables are separate owned data, not executable padding, and are not promoted by this retirement.
