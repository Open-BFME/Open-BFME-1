# Opaque Apt cleanup at 00897360

The recovered entry remains `rva00897360(bool)`. Neither a semantic function
name nor a new owner is asserted. Its direct calls reach the already matched
AptGC walkers at 00897260/008972B0 and three shutdown-chain bodies, followed
by a tail jump to the fourth. The retail log literal identifies the Apt GC
context without establishing the entry name.

## Extent and ABI

Retail begins at VA 00C97360. It reads the one-byte boolean stack argument,
retains no incoming ECX receiver, and ends with a JMP at RVA 0089745C to
008C3B60. That instruction ends at 00897461; INT3 padding continues through
0089746F. The complete extent is 257 bytes. Ghidra function creation reports
309 bytes because its reachable body includes tail-target code; that total
is not the contiguous entry extent. Ghidra read_memory independently agrees
with the retail bytes at the important unlink sequence.

The 008A30C0 hook consumes the receiver in ECX; its existing address-qualified
run pin is also used by landed Apt callers. The two walkers and four shutdown
callees use their ledger names. No new callee pins are needed.

## Source change

The old bank keeps the outer loop successor in ESI and reuses that register
for the unlink stores. Retail also copies ESI to EDX at +A5, then uses EDX
for the unlink while retaining ESI across virtual slot 12 for loop advance.
Copying the two-field intrusive header by value in the inline unlink helper
reproduces that independent temporary naturally. The links remain at item-8
and item-4, the layout used by the existing Bfme5FiftyFour.cpp provider.
No assembly, volatile access, or artificial register storage is involved.

The preferred old bank measured 255/257 bytes with 56 differences and only
19 relocations. Its historical callee-corrected alternative (attempt-history

measured 255/257 with 52 differences and 20 relocations. Applying the value
copy to that alternative produced all 257 bytes modulo relocation slots.
