# DX8Wrapper::End_Scene donor experiment

The tested revision is 575ba2b04743f190f069805fbdc59936123c45da. Open BFME 2's donor is Code/Libraries/Source/WWVegas/WW3D2/dx8wrapper.cpp at game.dat 0x00122BE0. It is a near twin, not a verbatim donor. Its separate texture assignment and texture setter supply a new inlining hypothesis. The hypothesis fails if the BFME 1 registration order and duplicated texture null checks remain different after porting those helpers.

The complete BFME 1 decode has no outgoing conditional branch. It returns at +0x267 and INT3 padding starts at +0x268. The matched WW3D::End_Render caller invokes DX8Wrapper::End_Scene(bool). D3D calls use stdcall slots +0xA8, +0x44 and +0x0C. Reset_Device receives one stack argument. The sleep helper at 0x009DB560 is a complete 12-byte cdecl wrapper around imported Sleep, not an unknown no-argument function. The texture release helper remains subject to the existing competing ledger identities.

The unwind map has one state. Its cleanup adjusts to the four-byte local and reaches the complete BfmeHandleCX destructor at 0x0005CC00 through ILT 0x00030652. That destructor tests its stored pointer and tail-calls texture release at 0x009EB7A0. The bank already represents this ownership and cleanup; the donor does not establish a different owner.

The unchanged bank emits 607 bytes with 175 non-relocation differences against the independently decoded 616-byte extent. The donor's /arch:SSE /G7 flags emit 573 bytes with 394 differences. Porting its separate assignment and setter emits 599 bytes with 184 differences. Combining that structure with its flags emits 569 bytes with 390 differences. The generated EH choices plateau after two unchanged alternatives; the generated frame-array choice is also unchanged. Every candidate still registers fs:[0] before reading the counter, unlike retail at +0x0E. Buffer cleanup scheduling and the second texture null test also remain different. These experiments refute the donor flags and helper separation as a remedy for this bank.

The existing bank is retained unchanged. Trial sources, complete retail decode, unwind output and unedited probe outputs are preserved under build/donor-retry-9/. Reopening requires compiler context that changes the first registration/global-load order or independently demonstrated helper visibility that preserves the second texture null check.
