# 0x00377060 CastleBehavior identity

The method spelling is not recovered. The safe identity is the address-derived
`CastleBehavior::rva00377060()`.

The retail body takes its receiver in ECX, saves it in ESI at +0x19, takes no
stack arguments, and ends with a plain `ret` at +0x3F0. Its complete code
extent is therefore 1009 bytes. The 1006-byte Ghidra/anchor extent cuts the
three-byte `add esp, 0x28` instruction at +0x3ED.

There is no direct call to the body because retail reaches it through ILT thunk
0x0001CD41. A raw image scan finds the call to that thunk at 0x0037791C. The
caller is state body 0x00377740 behind slot 0 of vtable 0x010E9B28. It forms
ECX as its state-subobject pointer minus 0x10 before the call, the same
CastleBehavior owner relationship independently documented by the matched
CastleBehavior constructor and `CastleBehavior::rva00371b00`.

The callee corroborates that owner: it reads the owner object at +0x08, an
ObjectID at +0xA0, and ObjectID vector ranges at +0xB8, +0xC4, and +0xDC. Its
retail trace says `CAMP: Frame %d: Castle ... Packed(aka:DIE)`. All 20 direct
callees resolve. The trace describes behavior but does not prove a source-level
method name, so the address token is intentionally retained.

`tools/name_oracle.py` currently has no witnessed CastleBehavior layout. This
identity therefore rests on the matched constructor/state-subobject caller and
the body/trace agreement, not on an invented field name.
