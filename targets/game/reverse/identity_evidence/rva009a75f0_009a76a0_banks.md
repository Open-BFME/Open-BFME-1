# Address-derived names for four-tap filter banks

These are partial attempts, not landed identities. Retail 0x009A75F0 ends with RET at 0x009A769D (174 bytes); retail 0x009A76A0 ends with RET at 0x009A7750 (177 bytes). Each has seven stack arguments, no calls and INT3-separated boundaries. Decoded source/destination loads and stores establish byte-to-unsigned-short and byte-to-int four-tap scalar loop shapes respectively.

The previous bank names bfmeFilter75F0 and bfmeGo7760 have no vendor, caller or symbol identity evidence. In particular, bfmeGo7760 embeds another address: 0x009A7760 is the next INT3-separated body after 0x009A76A0. The new names Rva009A75F0 and Rva009A76A0 state only the actual body address. Neither asserts a codec or original vendor function identity.

The improved sources preserve the independently decoded seven-argument cdecl ABI and complete extents. Exact scores remain below one because two final product additions swap order: 163/174 and 166/177 bytes. Bank history and re_log retain the measured results and earlier alternatives.
