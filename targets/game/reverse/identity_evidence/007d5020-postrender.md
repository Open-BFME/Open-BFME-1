# Partial reconstruction of 0x007D5020

The complete body is banked under the inherited `ScreenZoomFilter::postRender` name. That owner name is contradicted by the constructor-installed vtable and is retained only because an operator correction is required before renaming an existing bank. This is a partial reconstruction, not a source recovery. The investigation used revision `93d4657f320c662a58310715773c6d00d23380f6` and model `gpt-6.1-sol`.

## New hypothesis and refutation

The previous bank contained an entry skeleton, an invented unwind object and a by-value `Coord2D` argument. Newly landed sources for `Rva007D6B70` initialization, construction and mode setup, the grid helper at 0x007D4AF0, and the filter at 0x007D7910 supply independently checkable layout and dispatch evidence. The retry hypothesis was that reconstructing the actual four-argument interface, buffer ownership, native render-state inlines and complete shader constants would reproduce the exception frame and substantially improve on that skeleton. An independently decoded caller with another argument representation, a different constructor-installed slot target, or complete compiler experiments no closer than the saved skeleton would refute that hypothesis.

The saved skeleton and every trial were compiled against the full ledger extent. `build/007d5020/bank-baseline-raw.txt` preserves the original measurement. `build/007d5020/probe-final-names.txt` preserves the selected reconstruction measurement. The selected body's first unmasked difference is at offset 0x57. Its frame size and exception cleanup layout now agree with retail, while its instruction stream and full size do not. The score written by `re_log.py` is its measured score, not its normalized instruction-shape similarity.

## Boundary and owner

The full target interval is [0x007D5020, 0x007D5F2B). The two returns at offsets 0x54 and 0xF08 both pop sixteen bytes. All direct conditional branches remain inside the interval, and there is no outgoing tail jump. Every instruction and transfer is retained in `build/007d5020/retail.txt`; `checked-target.txt` contains the independent checked-callee inventory.

The matched constructor at 0x007D6B70 installs vtable VA 0x01128B24. Slot 3 points through ILT 0x0003F4AE to this target, and slots 5 and 6 supply its unadjusted `set` and `reset` calls. The owning address identity is therefore `Rva007D6B70`. The actual `ScreenZoomFilter` initializer is at 0x007D2390 and its post-render body is at 0x007D24A0; address-family order in the old verdict did not establish the assigned body's owner. `vtable.txt` retains the lookup. The existing constructor, initialization and mode-setup sources were read, rather than inferred from their ledger notes.

## Caller and indirect-call ABI

The complete dispatch wrapper at 0x00716A50 has extent 54 and forwards four stack words through virtual slot 3 with the selected filter in ECX. Its complete caller at 0x0073E050 ends at offset 0x1408 and has extent 5129, rather than the truncated ledger extent. At decoded caller offset 0x134, the caller pushes the display-size pair pointer, a byte flag pointer, a scroll-delta pair pointer, the mode word and filter index. It removes twenty bytes and tests AL. The wrapper consumes no stack arguments itself; the selected virtual removes the four forwarded words. There is no hidden return buffer or receiver adjustment.

The caller obtains the second argument from the complete `W3DView::calcDeltaScroll` helper at 0x0073B6B0. That helper writes both four-byte float fields, has a single complete return with RET 4, and receives the same output address subsequently passed to the wrapper. The assigned target does not read this argument. The fourth argument points to two adjacent float fields populated from display dimensions and read by the target. These observations support `postRender(FilterModes, Coord2D &, bool &, Coord2D *)`; RET 16 alone did not prove a by-value pair. The bank includes the canonical `Coord2D` header. `caller-complete.txt`, `wrapper-complete.txt`, `caller-scroll-output.txt` and their `checked-*-complete.txt` inventories preserve the complete bodies and aligned call sites.

The tactical-view slots 0x4C, 0x3C and 0x44 agree with the existing constructor-proven W3DView table and route to complete bodies 0x0045BA80, 0x0045BA30 and 0x0045BA50. The origin method writes two 32-bit integers and returns with RET 8; width and height return 32-bit integers. Their full decodes and checked inventories are `view-origin.txt`, `view-width.txt`, `view-height.txt` and `checked-view-*.txt`.

Device calls are retained as an opaque vtable slice. Their decoded slots and stack behavior support stdcall calls with an explicit receiver: render state at 0xE4, texture at 0x104, texture-stage state at 0x10C, sampler state at 0x114, immediate drawing at 0x14C, the legacy vertex-labelled operation at 0x164, pixel shader at 0x1AC and pixel constants at 0x1B4. The operation labels do not establish a canonical Direct3D interface version. Indirect return values are unused.

## Payloads, holders and ownership

The complete helper at 0x00719090 reads the actual sample view passed from receiver offset 0x2C. It reads begin and end pointers, advances by twelve bytes, and reads each element's fields at offsets 0, 4 and 8 as floats. Its first stack argument is also read as a float. The inherited pinned declaration accepts an unsigned word, so the candidate passes the exact 0.8f bit pattern through that existing declaration; the declaration is not evidence that the semantic argument is unsigned. `highlight-retail.txt` and `checked-highlight.txt` retain the whole helper. No STL container identity, key type, constructor or symbol is introduced. The view is passed opaquely, and no allocation-size inference supplies its element fields.

The target copies all three dwords from the accessor's groups at offsets 0x08 and 0x14 before uploading them with a fourth constant. The accessor at 0x00421E70 is a complete six-byte return of the recorded global base. Its complete decode is `grid-accessor.txt`. Scalar reads at offsets 0x00, 0x04, 0x20, 0x24, 0x4C, 0x50 and 0x54 are accounted for. The grid helper's complete decode and checked inventory establish the receiver fields at 0x20, 0x24 and 0x28 and its eleven explicit arguments. The selected source reuses that helper's existing pin and declarations.

The texture accessor at 0x0090DC60 takes the address of each four-byte holder at receiver offsets 0x10 and 0x14. It dereferences that holder, invokes virtual initialization checks on the held object and returns the nested native texture pointer. The members are not embedded `TextureBaseClass` objects. `texture-accessor.txt` and `checked-texture.txt` retain both complete return paths and the indirect calls.

The complete dynamic-index constructor at 0x0091D950 reads two 16-bit argument values in separate stack words and returns with RET 8. It establishes the type word, count at offset 4 and buffer pointer at offset 8. The complete box-vertex constructor at 0x0091F730 reads four stack words, including a 16-bit count at offset 0x10, and returns with RET 16. Its other written offsets are 0, 4, 8, 0x0C and 0x14. Both complete destructors were inspected, including every return path and indirect reference-release call. Their decodes are `ib-ctor-retail.txt`, `ib-dtor-retail.txt`, `vb-ctor-retail.txt` and `vb-dtor-retail.txt`, with matching checked inventories.

Retail has twelve exception unwind states. States 0 through 7 destroy independent temporary strings. State 8 destroys the index access object; state 9 destroys the vertex access object and proceeds to state 8. States 10 and 11 destroy strings and proceed to state 9. The selected candidate reproduces all predecessors and all cleanup-object offsets, including the second string slot. Its cleanup relocations name the string, index and vertex destructors. The retail string cleanup thunk at 0x0013A050 tail-jumps to the complete string-release body at 0x009DB7A0, which was decoded through both returns. `eh-retail.txt`, `eh-candidate-complete.txt`, `string-cleanup-thunk.txt`, `string-cleanup-body.txt` and their checked inventories preserve this evidence. Cleanup layout agreement is separate from the failed main-body byte check.

## Experiments and rejected hypotheses

All trial sources, raw probes, object receipts and the running measurements remain under `build/007d5020/` and the repository experiment store. The EH choices generator and shape search were run, followed by separate generated `throw()` trials for the accessor, grid update and highlight helper. None of those three declarations changed the chosen body's bytes. The non-EH family generator offered only a width-array alternative; its measured result was worse. Search manifests and unedited output are `eh-search-raw.txt` and `family-search-raw.txt`.

Native four-float copy semantics, raw position casts, scalar shader arrays, declaration order, a grouped local record, whole-constant assignments, temporary constant arguments and a visible noinline copy of the accessor were measured. Temporary shader constants reduced the frame too far; the visible accessor allowed repeated calls to disappear and moved further from retail. The latest landed filter's derived position-value pattern was also compiled and was worse. These are retained as trials 01 through 24, rather than being repeated without new evidence.

Two semantic corrections were established during complete stack tracking. The final draw condition reads the value uploaded from global offset 0x04, not the earlier amplitude. Trials 01 through 10 consequently contain a rejected condition and must not be preferred despite their occasional better positional score. Retail also multiplies by cached dimension reciprocals and reuses the normalized coordinate values; the selected candidate includes that structure. Scoping the named shader constants separately reproduces the retail frame and cleanup offsets. Snapshotting the highlight flag before writing the output flag preserves the decoded read-before-write behavior, but its load scheduling remains the first divergence.

## Remaining work and validation

`gate-candidate-final.txt` is the normal repository function verifier applied to a read-only temporary candidate row, without changing `functions.csv`. It fails byte comparison. The later string, constant, DIR32 and body checks are therefore not claimed to pass. No full gate is required for a bank and evidence-only change. `pin-consistency.txt` passes without new pins. Final CSV output is `check-csv-final.txt`.

The exploratory source-claim and class checks reject the scratch candidate because it owns no matched ledger row. The raw name snapshot comparison reports `DX8Wrapper` against the new tactical-view proxy after adoption of `dx8wrapper.h`; the source still uses the canonical existing `DX8Wrapper`, and no class was renamed to that proxy. These outputs are retained in `declared-unmatched.txt`, `class-gate.txt` and `name-regression.txt`. They are not a landing certificate.

The remaining codegen problem is local placement and scheduling after the matching frame, beginning with the output-pointer and highlight-flag loads at offset 0x57. The two byte globals at VAs 0x01307280 and 0x01307281 have no recorded DIR32 names in this revision. Their address-derived declarations in this bank are unresolved global identities for a future strict landing. The original owner name also requires an operator correction before a source claim. Reopening is justified by new evidence for the remaining local value/lifetime structure or the flag-load expression, independently supported names for those globals, and the owner correction. Repeating the recorded EH switches, temporary constants, accessor visibility or declaration-order trials provides no new experiment.
