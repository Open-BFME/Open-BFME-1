# RVA 0x00891AA0: opaque allocator-table forwarder

The retail baseline has INT3 padding at 0x00891A98..0x00891A9F,
then this complete 16-byte body:

```text
00891AA0  mov eax,[esp+4]
00891AA4  mov ecx,[0x01337A30]
00891AAA  push eax
00891AAB  call dword ptr [ecx+4]
00891AAE  pop ecx
00891AAF  ret
```

A distinct accessor starts at 0x00891AB0. The ledger's int3-carved
16-byte extent therefore agrees with independently decoded retail bytes.
`tools/callees.py 0x00891AA0 16` finds no direct calls; the sole indirect
call is through slot +4 of the existing `g_rva01337A30AllocPair` datum.
Its data row and Apt.cpp definition independently establish a pointer to
the two-slot allocator table, whose second slot accepts a storage pointer.
No new global or pin is needed.

A raw search of the retail image found no absolute pointer to VA
0x00C91AA0 and no executable-section E8/E9 displacement targeting its RVA.
There is consequently no caller or label establishing a semantic method
name, owner, or original formal-parameter count. `Rva00891AA0` retains the
address and claims none of those identities. Placement in Apt.cpp follows
the allocator datum already owned by that TU, not a new file-name claim.

## Source shape and limits

The C++ function uses cdecl, reads the one observed pointer argument, and
leaves stack cleanup to its caller. Its second unsigned formal is unused:
retail does not read it, and its presence is **not** asserted as an original
signature discovery. It is an ABI-compatible source representation of this
otherwise unreferenced body. An extra incoming cdecl slot has no observable
effect when it is neither read nor callee-popped.

Under the repository's VC7.1 flags, a one-formal forwarder becomes a 9-byte
tail jump; an unused second formal emits the exact 16-byte call/pop/ret.
A variadic declaration still emits 9 bytes; `_ReadWriteBarrier` and
`_WriteBarrier` emit 18 bytes with `add esp,4`; disabling global
optimization emits 21 bytes with a frame pointer. The committed body needs
no barrier, assembly, or optimization override. This compiler experiment
proves the emitted shape, **not** a sized-delete identity or exact original
formal count.
