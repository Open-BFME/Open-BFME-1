## Boundary and incoming ABI

The complete 1356-byte WinMain body at RVA `0x000600F0` contains the actual instruction-aligned call at `0x000604F8`, not a raw opcode coincidence. This proves the incoming HINSTANCE in EDI, the two stack words in show-command then byte-flag order, caller stack cleanup, and a byte return. EDI is read but never changed by the initializer. The complete caller's outgoing branches and common return were reviewed in `checked_caller.raw.txt` and the full decode.

The support caller's entire emitted 25-byte body is retained in `object_final.raw.txt`. It loads HINSTANCE into EDI, pushes the flag and show command in that order, calls the recovered body, removes eight bytes, restores EDI, and returns AL unchanged. It is marked `absent-from-retail`, exists to retain MSVC 7.1's private static convention, and is not claimed as a retail recovery. The mangled ordinary C++ parameter declaration alone does not describe this compiler-private convention.
