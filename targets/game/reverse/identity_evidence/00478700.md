# GameWindow text-colour getters at 0x00478700..0x00478770

The eight 7-byte bodies from `0x00478700` to `0x00478770` are the eight
`GameWindow::winGet*TextColor` / `*BorderColor` accessors, not the Object
accessors five of them were ledgered as.

| body | reads | old row | identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| `0x00478700` | `+0x18C` | `Object::getBehaviorModules` | `winGetEnabledTextColor` |
| `0x00478710` | `+0x190` | (already this name) | `winGetEnabledTextBorderColor` |
| `0x00478720` | `+0x194` | `Object::getBodyModule` | `winGetDisabledTextColor` |
| `0x00478730` | `+0x198` | `Object::getStealth` | `winGetDisabledTextBorderColor` |
| `0x00478740` | `+0x1A4` | `Gen_00478740::m` | `winGetIMECompositeTextColor` |
| `0x00478750` | `+0x1A8` | `Object::friend_getRadarData` | `winGetIMECompositeBorderColor` |
| `0x00478760` | `+0x19C` | `Gen_00478760::m` | `winGetHiliteTextColor` |
| `0x00478770` | `+0x1A0` | `Object::getPhysics` | `winGetHiliteTextBorderColor` |

Four independent checks agree:

1. Order. `GameWindow.cpp` defines the getters as enabled colour, enabled
   border, disabled colour, disabled border, IME colour, IME border, hilite
   colour, hilite border, and retail lays the run out in that order.
2. Offsets. Each body reads Zero Hour's `m_instData` text field plus 4, the
   BFME-only insertion at `+0x04` that `inputs/reference/shims/gamewindow` and the
   landed getters of 54f49fb812e measure; `0x00478710` was already matched
   that way.
3. Pins. The ILT thunks of six bodies are pinned under these GameWindow names
   (`0x0002A5D1`, `0x0002D7EA`, `0x0002D4E8`, `0x0002C55C`, `0x0000A128`,
   `0x00019961`).
4. Callers. The 123 retail calls into the run come from 21 functions: 16
   named GUI, gadget-draw and load-screen functions, none of them an Object
   method, and five anonymous dumps. `d_00798F30` and `d_007991A0` each call
   the eight bodies 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 2, 1, 1 times, which is the exact call
   profile of Zero Hour's `W3DGadgetTextEntryDraw` and
   `W3DGadgetTextEntryImageDraw`; that fixes the two unpinned IME bodies.

The Object names cannot hold in BFME: `docs/object_layout.md` places
`m_body` at `+0x200`, `m_physics` at `+0x208` and `m_radarData` at `+0x20C`
and records `m_stealth` as gone, and `targets/game/reverse/bfme_layouts.json` witnesses
`m_behaviors` at `+0x1F0` (15 of 16 votes). Each body now byte-matches from
`GameWindow.cpp` through `BFME_WIN_AT`.
