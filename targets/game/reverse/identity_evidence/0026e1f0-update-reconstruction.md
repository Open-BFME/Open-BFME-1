# WoundArrowUpdate update reconstruction

The complete update body has a measured C++ reconstruction, but its byte gate fails. The retained candidate is a partial, and no ledger recovery is claimed. The tested checkout revision is `59a7d205747039f201c47a160ed94987c1003971`. The experiment source is `build/woundarrow-0026e1f0/trial12.cpp`; its resolved comparison and exact differing offsets are retained in `build/woundarrow-0026e1f0/measurements.json`. Raw probe, scoped gate, decoded instructions and checked-callee outputs remain in the same task directory. This investigation used `gpt-6.1-sol`.

## New evidence and retry hypothesis

Earlier attempts produced no saved body and stopped at the AI virtual contracts, primary callback and integer argument passed through a pointer-typed adapter. The current ledger contains independent source for `AIUpdateInterface::isIdle() const` and `AIUpdateInterface::chooseLocomotorSet(LocomotorSetType)`. The hypothesis was that these declarations and canonical Object fields permit a complete reconstruction without changing shared headers or adding pins. A different decoded vtable target, receiver adjustment, argument width or stack cleanup would refute that hypothesis. The subsequent compiler experiments refute exactness of the tested shapes, rather than the target's boundary or those direct-call contracts.

The current neighbour sources were read directly: `S3SeedPairsGuarded.cpp`, `Bfme5Forty.cpp` and `Rva0026E380.cpp`. None of those names establishes a type independently of the instructions described below.

Checked-callee logs cover the target and all directly used helpers, including the indirect-call implementations used as ABI evidence. The constructor and name-key getter are retained as complete decoded bodies. This note does not certify every dynamic subclass override reached by the engine.

Two applicability limits remain explicit for a future collector: the AI constructor's unwind cleanup was not independently reconstructed, and the Contain slot's dynamic implementation was not decoded here. The constructor is used only to identify installed tables and base offsets, while the existing integer-bit forwarding adapter remains unchanged. Treat either broader ownership or dynamic-callee claim as unresolved until its own evidence is retained. Neither limit is excused by the partial's score.

## Measured experiments and stopping condition

The first complete source cached the adjusted primary receiver across the branches, adding a frame slot and early register save. Repeating the receiver adjustment at each use removed that lifetime difference. Qualifying the final onExit call as a direct call removed the virtual-dispatch mismatch. The ordinary virtual-call version then differed only around the movement arithmetic because its AI vptr load was scheduled late. Canonical coordinate/header adoption, authentic lookup visibility and a constructor-proven secondary command base left that outcome unchanged.

The finite family search preserved its generated choices, every candidate and raw probes under `build/shape_search/c52a1f4a14654b3c8e663d946ef6cbc3` and `build/woundarrow-0026e1f0/search-probe*.txt`. Reversing the two coordinate stores made the result worse. `/Oa` made the result worse, and `/G6` did not change the native virtual-call result. A typed vtable view moved the vptr load to the retail position but changed the register assignment and the order of the two multiplication operands. The member-pointer version emitted the same instructions as the two-register fastcall codegen view, so further unchanged register or x87 experiments stopped. A free-function `__thiscall` pointer spelling was rejected by VC7.1 with C4234 and was not treated as a measured body.

The retained member-pointer view uses the compiler's single-inheritance member-pointer representation solely as a codegen adapter. It does not prove that the engine's vtable stores C++ member-pointer objects. Its slot offset and native receiver/enum/return contracts are established independently above. The canonical private onExit declaration was then adopted, and the ordinary scoped byte gate resolved every direct call without adding a pin. The exact remaining mismatch is recorded by the resolved comparison, rather than estimated from an instruction-normalized score.

Reopening is justified by a concrete explanation for the native vptr-load scheduling or for the explicit table view's register assignment and multiplication order. Repeating the rejected receiver cache, lookup visibility, command-base, store reversal or `/G6` spellings without additional evidence does not supply a new experiment. The ordinary virtual-call alternative remains `build/woundarrow-0026e1f0/trial08.cpp` for comparison with the bank.
