The 131-byte body in `game/GameEngine/Source/GameLogic/Object/Update/AIUpdate/Rva002BC260GoalSetup.cpp` has the existing pinned identity `?run@Rva002BC260GoalOwner@@QAEXPAX00E@Z`. Its prior ledger spelling, `?run@Rva002BC260Owner@@QAEXPAX000@Z`, describes the fourth argument as a pointer. The address-derived owner and method retain their opaque meaning; this correction adopts the existing pin and the retail byte contract rather than asserting an EA class name.

The matched callers `GiantBirdFollowPathState::rva002BDBF0` at RVA 0x002BDBF0 and `GiantBirdFollowWaypointPathState::updateWaypointGoal` at RVA 0x002BF0C0 pass their AI receiver in ECX and call ILT RVA 0x0000795A. They push EAX without clearing its upper bits. Only the low byte of that fourth slot is a defined flag value; the slot does not represent a pointer.

At RVA 0x002BC260 the receiver is used without adjustment. The body loads its fourth stack slot from `[esp+0x10]` and forwards that word as the sixth argument to ILT RVA 0x0000A795, which jumps to RVA 0x00149470. The owner constructs the goal receiver at AI offset 0x400, supplies the other five arguments, copies coordinates from AI offset 0x454 to 0x47C, clears fields at 0x468 and 0x46C, and ends with RET 16. This is one member body with three pointer arguments and one byte argument occupying four x86 stack slots.

The complete 1943-byte initializer at RVA 0x00149470 first consumes the sixth input at RVA 0x001494B0 with `mov al, byte ptr [esp+0x88]`, then tests AL. Its prologue accounts for 0x70 bytes of stack displacement, so this operand is the sixth argument at entry offset 0x18. Later wider accesses to that stack location occur after the incoming flag has been consumed and the slot reused. Both return paths use RET 24. The owner's local initializer declaration and member-pointer type therefore use `unsigned char` for the sixth argument as well.
