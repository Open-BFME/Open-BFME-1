The recovered symbol is `?update@Rva003A2070Obj@@QAEXM@Z`. The owner remains address-derived. `update`, `m_accum`, `m_begin`, `m_end` and the element's `m_body` retain the saved attempt's descriptive names. No semantic class identity, pair key, allocator identity or vector ownership is claimed by the source's range view.

## Boundary and ABI

Retail starts at RVA `0x003A2070` and ends immediately after `ret 4` at `0x003A2201`, with padding from `0x003A2204`. Every conditional branch stays within the 404-byte extent. The size guard joins the epilogue at `0x003A21FD`; the failed helper test joins at `0x003A21FA` after the additional saved registers have been pushed. The loop back edge targets `0x003A2170`. There are no outgoing tail jumps or exception states in this target.

The target reads one four-byte float at entry stack offset `+4`, retains incoming ECX in ESI, returns no value and removes four argument bytes. It loads double `15.0` from VA `0x010EC708`, calls the IAT thunk at RVA `0x009F70CC` for `_CIfmod`, subtracts the result from the input and stores the float at receiver offset `+0x18`. The loop adds float `15.0` from VA `0x010888F0`. These literal values were read from retail, rather than inferred from the old bank.

## Calls and indirect dispatch

The five-byte ILT at RVA `0x000481FD` jumps to the complete 566-byte body at RVA `0x003A1A30`. That body reads its first argument as the object pointer, reads only the low byte of argument two as a flag, and uses argument three as an optional float output pointer. Incoming ECX is not read before being overwritten. Every return writes AL and executes `ret 12`: the early failure at `+0x36`, the exhausted-range failure at `+0x153`, and success at `+0x233`. The source keeps a member-call view to preserve the caller's ECX load and argument ordering, and calls the existing address-derived thunk symbol. It adds no pin and gives the helper no semantic identity.

Complete decoded callers at RVAs `0x00148800` (124 bytes) and `0x00741360` (545 bytes) independently confirm the helper's argument order, low-byte flag, receiver load and result test. Raw call searches were validated at decoded instruction boundaries in those complete bodies. The additional caller at `0x00149470` was retained and checked through its complete 1943-byte extent, rather than Ghidra's shorter inventory extent. ## Experiments and refutation

The saved source emits only 29 of 404 bytes and has seventeen differing emitted bytes. A complete direct-field reconstruction emitted 384 bytes. A concrete manager view removed generalized member-pointer branches; separating the range's `size` and indexing accessors reproduced the prefix and both repeated size calculations. The resulting full-size candidate had 76 differing bytes. Moving the flag update before the terrain call reduced this to 49. A reference getter and separating initialization from assignment did not fix the first copy. The donor `Coord3D::set` field-copy operation reduced the mismatch to six bytes; defining the initial flag before that coordinate copy removed the final adjacent scheduling swap. The canonical coordinate header and an address-derived helper receiver retained the exact result.

Raw immutable trial sources and probe outputs are retained in `build/rva003a2070/`, including `probe00_saved.log`, `probe01.log` through `probe08.log` and `probe_final_source.log`. Complete instruction evidence is in `retail_decode.log`, `retail_additional.log`, `helper_copy_complete.log` and `callers_decode.log`; checked-callee results are in `callees_evidence.log`. These logs are local handoff evidence, not a claim that relocation masking proves identity. A decoded branch leaving the stated extent, a disagreeing helper return cleanup, or a field access inconsistent with the exposed coordinate offsets would refute the corresponding reconstruction claim.
