# RVA 0x008D1CA0 allocation and unwind retry

The body remains unmatched. Correcting the final allocation improves the preferred bank from diagnostic quality 0.4248 to 0.6062, but that bank still has an incorrect constructor-failure cleanup. A separate native-allocation reconstruction has the correct cleanup and local-frame size, but its quality is 0.3508. Neither is an exact recovery. All measurements used the 419-byte retail extent at revision `91ced6e6f0a9d577b05b6c510820078adfe345c3` with model `gpt-6.1-sol`.

## New hypothesis and refutation

The previous stack-slot blocker concealed two allocation contracts. The saved reconstruction uses placement construction after a headered allocation, while retail's exception actions free the allocation through a sized class delete. Retail also uses the raw allocator for the final four-byte block. The hypothesis was that restoring native `new` and the witnessed sized delete would remove the placement temporary and recover the four-byte local frame. An unchanged eight-byte frame or unchanged placement cleanup would refute that hypothesis. The native experiment recovered both, but did not recover the complete instruction stream.

The existing holder constructor, headered allocator, sized delete, tagged-pointer setters, and stack-pop helper were inspected directly. They provide physical layouts and callable contracts; their names are not independent native class identities. No shared header was found for the bank's views. No source or pin whose name contains `_STL` was added or changed. The available Zero Hour reference contains no AptActionInterpreter source donor.

## Allocation, members and ownership

The two allocations at target offsets `+0x98` and `+0xDB` call the 33-byte helper at `0x00897560` with size `0x1C`. That helper requests eight additional bytes from the allocator at VA `0x01337828`, advances past the header, links the allocation through `0x00897300`, and returns the adjusted pointer. The final allocation at target `+0x14C` calls the allocator pointer directly with size four. The old bank incorrectly calls the headered helper at that site. The improved preferred bank corrects this call using the existing `Rva008C5D70Alloc` declaration and pin.

These are physical access and ownership witnesses, not scalar-value or STL type deductions. The tagged setters at `0x00899320` and `0x008992C0` read and replace the fields at twelve and eight respectively, retaining the incoming value and releasing the old value. All return paths were inspected.

## Measured experiments and stopping condition

The original bank compiles to 418 bytes, with 239 differing non-relocation bytes, first at `+0x15`; repository quality is 0.4248. Correcting the final allocator produces 419 bytes, 165 differing bytes, first at `+0x15`, and quality 0.6062. It retains six relocation sites that do not align with retail operands and an eight-byte local frame. The native-allocation variant produces 417 bytes, 268 differing bytes, first at `+0x16`, and quality 0.3508, including the repository's doubled size-error penalty. Its local frame is four bytes and its unwind cleanup agrees with retail. Normalized instruction similarity is 0.973, but that is not byte equality.

Raw probes and complete sources are retained under `build/8d1ca0/`. The final measured snapshots are `018-best-distance.cpp` and `019-native-ownership.cpp`, with their `.probe.log` files. `001-byte-gate.log` and `013-byte-gate.log` are raw scoped calls to `build.verify_functions` using candidate rows; both fail with exit status one. Their relocation diagnostics are positional after instruction-layout drift and are not evidence of new callee addresses.

The generated EH search tested eight variants and found no changed output. Its manifest and per-trial raw probes are under `build/shape_search/0b39b003e7da432b996971f93871b836/`. The generated family search tested four frame variants and found no changed output; its evidence is under `build/shape_search/4982186661b44f9c8b90ae8d66b04800/`. The two ordinary register trials, changing the initial load order and a held-pointer spelling, were unchanged and were not continued as a register search.

Additional measured hypotheses covered native allocation, direct constructor-result lifetimes, tagged-member accessor visibility, a tagged-word wrapper, unified value views, owned placement construction, removal of the obsolete new header, visible stack access and pop helpers, and nested lexical scopes. The tagged accessor, wrapper and owned-placement variants changed shape but worsened the measured result. The remaining native variants reproduced the same 417-byte output. These trials reject unchanged source spellings as a useful next experiment. Reopening requires new evidence for the native tagged-access or allocation expression that changes the missing EAX-to-EBX copy and register lifetimes, while retaining the witnessed sized cleanup; repeating declaration order is not justified.
