## BFME behavior recovered

The routine refuses absent profiles, absent slots, observers and games with no other human. BFME uses a second slot loop plus GameLogic+290 to recognize disconnect endings, and checks player defeat as well as end-of-game flags. A successful local winner sends request kind 6; other players send kind 7. Disconnected games stop after this submission. Missing cached statistics submit only the CRC mismatch update.

The BFME faction map is Gondor=1, Rohan=0, Isengard=3, Mordor=2. Outcome/streak/game maps use the faction index; score totals, duration and game-size maps use the PlayerTemplate index. One-versus-one and two-versus-two ranks and last ladder names are updated. Duration uses the two unsigned divisions visible in retail. The routine submits a kind-1 statistics request, tracks and publishes the resulting statistics, caches their serialized form in GameSpyMiscPreferences, and updates GameSpyInfo's cached local statistics.
