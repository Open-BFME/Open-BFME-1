# Matrix3 overloads from Matrix3D and Matrix4 (0x008D8F80 to 0x008D9190)

`Matrix3(const Matrix3D&)` is at 0x008D8F80 and `Matrix3(const Matrix4&)` at 0x008D8FC0; the ledger had them the other way round. By the same evidence `Set(const Matrix4&)` is at 0x008D9040, which the ledger named `Set(const Matrix3D&)` although that overload is proven at 0x008D9000. The bodies of each Matrix3D/Matrix4 pair compile to identical bytes, so the bytes cannot decide; the call sites and the retail layout do.

## Retail layout

Retail keeps the definition order of `inputs/reference/CnC_Generals_Zero_Hour/GeneralsMD/Code/Libraries/Source/WWVegas/WWMath/matrix3.cpp` for this object: the constructors from Matrix3D and Matrix4, `Set` from Matrix3D, Matrix4 and Quaternion, `operator=` from Matrix3D and Matrix4, then the two `Multiply` overloads with a Matrix3D operand. The ledger rows at 0x008D8F80, 0x008D8FC0, 0x008D9000, 0x008D9040, 0x008D9080 (`Set(const Quaternion&)`), 0x008D9150, 0x008D9190, 0x008D91D0 and 0x008D92C0 follow that order. Where a pair is decided by matched callers below, the first address of the pair is the Matrix3D overload.

## Call sites

All eight retail call sites into these six addresses are direct `call rel32`; no call reaches them through an incremental-link entry.

- 0x008D8F80 has four callers, and each passes a 0x30-byte Matrix3D.
  - 0x005F7248 in 0x005F6ED0 passes the local at `esp+0x5C`, which 0x005F716B to 0x005F71E5 fill with twelve dwords copied from `[eax+0xC0]` to `[eax+0xEC]`. The Matrix3 under construction starts at `esp+0x8C`, directly after those 0x30 bytes, and 0x005F7524 later passes the same local to vtable slot +0x54 of the object at `[esi+0x70]`.
  - 0x007362FD in 0x00736150 passes tree record +0x10. The matched `W3DTreeBuffer` addTree at 0x00736B60 fills that field from its `const Matrix3D *` parameter, and the record's tree type follows at +0x40.
  - 0x00720465 in 0x007202F0 passes shrub record +0x10 (`[esi+0x1558]`, with the records starting at +0x1548).
  - 0x0061D3FB in 0x0061D3E0 passes its own parameter. That function has no call site, incremental-link caller or data reference, and its `Matrix4` parameter type had been taken from the old callee name, so it carries no evidence either way; it now takes a Matrix3D.
- 0x008D8FC0 has one caller, 0x005F721D in 0x005F6ED0, which passes the local at `esp+0x108`. The same function fills that local through the D3DX9 dispatch slots 0x012DBE70, 0x012DBE74, 0x012DBE78 and 0x012DBEA0, whose matched vendored initializers are `init_D3DXMatrixRotationX`, `Y`, `Z` and `Axis`. Those write a 4x4 `D3DXMATRIX`, so 0x008D8FC0 is the Matrix4 overload.
- 0x008D9000 is called by the matched `camera.cpp` (`NearClipBBox.Basis.Set(cam_mat)`) and `boxrobj.cpp` (`CachedBox.Basis.Set(Transform)`), both with a Matrix3D, as `caller-decided-2.md` already recorded. 0x008D9040 has no call site; it is the remaining `Set` twin, at the place the definition order gives `Set(const Matrix4&)`.
- 0x008D9150 is called by the matched `mesh.cpp` (`OBBoxIntersectionTestClass` from an AABox test and a Matrix3D), recorded in `caller-decided-2.md`. 0x008D9190 has no call site and sits where the order puts `operator=(const Matrix4&)`; its placeholder row is left as it is.
