## Copy-forwarding: the mechanism is found, but the slot order is still wrong

- **Measured:** the in-if read comes from `first`'s slot, as in retail, whenever `first` is
  accessed both as a block and field-wise between `first = second` and the in-if copy. Use a
  POD `BfmeVec2EV`, as in the landed helper TU `BfmeConv2043.cpp`, with one of those two
  copies field-wise and the other a struct assignment. A `memcpy` or a POD-cast block copy at
  either site does the same.
  - All-member-wise copies (bank) are forwarded to `second`.
  - All-block POD copies keep `second.x/y` in registers, giving 1236 diffs.
  - Example: `build/wf/0058e390/AI_pod.cpp` has 26 diffs. The 0x114 site is fixed, but
    `first` and `imageSize1` exchange frame slots (12 lea/mov displacement bytes).
- **Measured (`/FAsc`):** VC7.1 orders the 8-byte objects by reference weight, with heavier
  objects at lower addresses. In the mixed form `first` outweighs `imageSize1` by about 2
  references: adding two `imageSize1` reads flips them, one does not.
  - The forwarded form drops `first`'s two in-if reads, which is why the bank's slots match
    retail.
  - Retail therefore needs about 2 more `imageSize1` references (or 2 fewer `first`
    references) that leave no code.
- **Ruled out for the slot order:**
  - All 3x3x3 combinations of block, field-wise, user `operator=`, constructor-form and POD-cast
    copies at the pre, mid and in-if sites.
  - Inline copy helpers taking references or pointers.
  - Declaration-order permutations.
  - `imageSize1` declared at the top.
  - Dead initial stores (DSE runs before weighting).
  - Pointer and reference aliases for `first` (`imageSize2 = &first`).
  - Block scopes starting at text1, text2, the if, y or rowHeight, and an if/else body.
  - A separate `imageSize2` local. `image2`, not `imageSize2`, then takes `first`'s dead slot,
    because `first` is address-taken and function-scope.
  - Block-scoped `first`.
  - A `float[2]` representation.
