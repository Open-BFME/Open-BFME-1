  `build/rva00778590-nothrow1.txt`,
  `build/rva00778590-nothrow1-shape.txt`. The flip is not suppressible by
  local zero-constant control because it starts in the prologue.

So neither compare spelling matches; the retail compare is evidently a
third shape (specification or TU context we have not reproduced). Both

## The 80 remaining blocks are regalloc/scheduling residue

Full list: `build/rva00778590-shape-diff.txt`. Classification:

- [0/1] setName receiver load scheduling (`mov ecx,[esi+8]` placed
  differently around the lea/push of the +0x3C temp).
- [2-7] downstream of the compare/nothrow delta (EH state 2 insert,
  `jne`/`je` pairing, `[esp+0x194]` 1/0 vs ebp, `test ecx` vs
  `cmp ecx,ebp`).
- [8/38-40] dead-slot spills (retail `mov [esp+0x14],eax`; our
  `mov ebx,[esi+8]` + `[esp+0x24],ebx` + `xor edi,edi`).
- [9/10/17/18/44-46/62-70] argument load/store ordering: retail keeps arg3
  in ebx across a wide window (+0xA9..+0xF07) and keeps m_38 in ebx; our
  build reloads/re-spills (`[esp+0x1a0]`/`[esp+0x1a4]`,
  `[esp+0x3C]` string temps, `[esp+0x14]`/`[esp+0x18]`/`[esp+0x20]`/
  `[esp+0x24]`/`[esp+0x1c]`).
- [11-16/21/24-27/31/34-37/64-70] register lifetime: retail holds values
  in ebx/ebp across the windows above (`shr ebp,0x1f` arithmetic form,
  `mov ecx,ebx` vs `lea ecx,[esi+0x38]`, `mov ecx,[esp+...]` vs
  register forms).
- [19/20] special-model test: retail `cmp edx,[eax+0x88]` (memory form)
  with the `add eax,0x84` deferred to a second use; ours materialises
  `mov edx,[eax+0x88]; add eax,0x84; cmp ecx,edx`.
- [22/23/32/33] `[esp+0x3C]` string-temp lea placement (four sites).
- [47/57] branch codegen: retail `lea edx,[eax+8]; jne` vs our
  `je; add edx,8; jmp` (two sites).
- [71/72] movzx placement: retail `mov al,[eax+0x130]; movzx eax,al`
  vs our single `movzx edx,[edx+0x130]`.
- [76-79] tail register choice (`esi+0x34` in ecx vs eax;
  `esi+0x4c`/`esi+0x50` cmp in edx vs eax/edx ordering).

None of these has a clean, evidence-backed source lever: they are
callee-saved register ownership and scheduling decisions the MSVC
frontend made differently under the compare-specification cascade. A
source rewrite that changes ownership in one window perturbs the others.
