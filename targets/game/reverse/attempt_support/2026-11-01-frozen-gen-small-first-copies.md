## What blocks ranks 1 and 13

Both are `losers` rows whose retail-verified copy sits in a clean TU
(`RvaDequeReallocateMap.cpp`, `stlport_vector_voidptr_members.cpp`) and whose
kept copy is a `game/gen_small/tgrid_NNN.cpp` explicit instantiation:

* rank 1 `deque<Gen_t_00593270_p12cd>::_M_reallocate_map`: kept holder
  `tgrid_134.obj` (digest ac55938231b1, 360 B, memmove calls shaped differently
  from the retail 363 B body 0x00591230; ledger row `RvaDequeReallocateMap.cpp`,
  verdict retail c1bf4d7f6787).
* rank 13 `vector<void*>::erase`: kept holder `tgrid_000.obj` (digest
  1b78c44859c3, wrong); retail row 0x000CF750 in
  `stlport_vector_voidptr_members.cpp` (b805076c2654). A second non-frozen
  emitter, `stlport_vector_voidptr_fill_assign.obj` (aa3292b21fd6, wrong), is
  also in the census; it is not what keeps the link on a wrong copy.

link.exe keeps the first copy in link order and `link_census.link_order`
places an object at the lowest RVA among its matched rows. `tgrid_134.obj`
spans 0x00359790..0x0063C190 and `tgrid_000.obj` starts lower still, so each
sorts before the clean TU (first row 0x00591230 / 0x000CF750). The clean TU's
copy is byte-exact against retail and can never win while those objects keep
rows. Their sources are `// Generated by: python3 tools/gen_small.py gen-tgrid`
and generators are retired (b437c337c2), `add_match.py` treats gen_small as
frozen, and retiring either object means replacing its 100 (tgrid_134) or 331
(tgrid_000) rows with clean C++.

Measured in this session (build outputs private to the worktree):

* rank 1: the tgrid_134 copy of the 12-byte-element `_M_reallocate_map`
  differs from retail starting at +0x25 (`jbe` offsets, then different
  memmove/copy call sequences); not fixable from the Rva TU.
* no source in the clean TU can change that digest; no `selected`/`comdat`
