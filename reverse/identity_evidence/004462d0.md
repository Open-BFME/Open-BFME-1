* **Slot.** Slot 56 (`+0xE0`) of InGameUI's primary vftable 0x00CF5B38 is the
  ILT thunk 0x00046100, which jumps to 0x004462D0. Slot 57 beside it is
  `deselectDrawable` (0x00446490, reverse/identity_evidence/00446490.md), as

  `m_selectedDrawables` (`+0x18`, `name_oracle` 1.00), increments the select
  count at `+0x554`, calls `evaluateSoloNexus(draw)` and
  `ControlBar::onDrawableSelected(draw)` (0x0049CB90, a named ledger row).

  "GateProxyBehavior", and skips the selection when 0x001FCA90 returns true.
  That query keeps its address-derived name `Rva001FCA90Owner::invoke`.
