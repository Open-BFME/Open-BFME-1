# Locomotor body at RVA 0x001B5A30

The previous `getMaxAcceleration` identity is not supported by the field
accesses. The retail BFME LocomotorTemplate parse table at RVA `0x00C9D860`
assigns `Lift` and `LiftDamaged` to template offsets `+0x44` and `+0x48`.
Retail instructions at `0x001B5A30` select those exact offsets according to
the passed damage state, multiply by the `Object` AI locomotor scale at
`Object+0x204` / AI `+0x1D4`, and clamp against `Locomotor+0x24`.

The byte-level calling convention is a Locomotor thiscall method: `ECX` is the
receiver, the first stack argument is `Object*`, the second is the damage-state
enum, and the body ends in `ret 8`. Thus the class and ABI are retained. The
clamp field at `+0x24` and exact method purpose remain unproven; the body may be
lift-related, but there is no named direct caller in the current caller index.
The old ledger note attributing a call to `0x001B80D0` conflicts with its
recorded analysis, which describes a hover/bob update and no query call. The
function therefore remains address-derived as `Locomotor::rva001B5A30`, and the
clamp field remains `m_value24` until better evidence establishes their names.
