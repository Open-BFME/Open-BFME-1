# Disconnect-frame and chat player-mask getters at 0x00673CA0 and 0x00673CB0

Both bodies are `mov eax,[ecx+0x20]; ret`. Retail was linked without
identical-COMDAT folding (`tiny-accessor-aliases.md`), so they are two
functions, and the ledger had them the wrong way round:

| body | old row | identity |
|---|---|---|
| `0x00673CA0` | `Gen_00673ca0::m` (scaffold) | `NetDisconnectPlayerCommandMsg::getDisconnectFrame` |
| `0x00673CB0` | `NetDisconnectPlayerCommandMsg::getDisconnectFrame` | `NetChatCommandMsg::getPlayerMask` |

1. Pins. The pinned `getDisconnectFrame` ILT `0x0003F62A` jumps to
   `0x00673CA0`; the pinned `NetChatCommandMsg::getPlayerMask` ILT
   `0x00002B1C` jumps to `0x00673CB0`.
2. Callers. `0x00673CA0` is called by `NetPacket::addDisconnectPlayerCommand`
   and the anonymous wire-record filler `0x00677280`. `0x00673CB0` is called
   by `NetPacket::addChatCommand`, `NetPacket::FillBufferWithChatCommand`
   and `BFMEConnectionManager::resolvePlayerFromName`, all chat paths.
3. Order. Retail lays out `NetDisconnectPlayerCommandMsg::setDisconnectSlot`
   (`0x00673C80`), `setDisconnectFrame` (`0x00673C90`), then `0x00673CA0`;
   `0x00673CB0` is followed by `NetChatCommandMsg::setPlayerMask`
   (`0x00673CC0`), which stores the same `+0x20`.

Both rows compile from `NetCommandMsg_ctors.cpp`, whose views already place
`m_disconnectFrame` and `m_playerMask` at `+0x20`.
