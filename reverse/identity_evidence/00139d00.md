# ThingFactory::parseObjectDefinition at 0x00139D00

Commit c0ad0b704f proved three things about the 541-byte body at 0x00139D00. It
is the shared body behind the Object, ObjectReskin and ChildObject INI blocks. It
is static, because its first argument is read off the stack and not from ecx. And
it is not ThingFactory::newDrawable. That commit gave the body the class qualifier
`INI::` but offered no evidence for that class. This file records the evidence for
`ThingFactory::` instead.

- **Zero Hour twin.** Zero Hour declares `static void
  ThingFactory::parseObjectDefinition(INI*, const AsciiString&, const
  AsciiString&)` in ThingFactory.h:97 and defines it in ThingFactory.cpp:372. Its
  INI::parseObjectDefinition block handler reads the name and calls
  ThingFactory::parseObjectDefinition. BFME's handlers at 0x000C27E0, 0x000C2870
  and 0x000C2930 have the same shape. The BFME body follows the same sequence:
  - findTemplate or newTemplate, then markAsOverride under INI_LOAD_CREATE_OVERRIDES
  - newOverride
  - copyFrom, setCopiedFromDefault and setReskinnedFrom for a reskin
  - initFromINI
  - "ObjectReskin must come after the original Object (%s, %s)."

  BFME adds a third name (childOf) and the ChildObject branch.
- **Placement.** The body lies inside ThingFactory.obj's contribution to the
  image, which contains:
  - the ThingFactory constructor 0x00138960
  - the deleting destructor 0x001384D0
  - newObject 0x00138520
  - the ThingTemplate copy-assignment at 0x00139070
  - newOverride 0x00139A80
  - newTemplate 0x00139B40

  After those comes 0x00139D00, and the INIException destructor its throws need
  follows at 0x00139FF0. Zero Hour's ThingFactory.cpp defines
  parseObjectDefinition after newTemplate and newOverride in the same way. The INI
  bodies are elsewhere: INI.cpp at 0x0085xxxx and INIObject.cpp at 0x000C27E0.
- **Access.** The body calls newOverride at 0x00139A80, which is matched as
  `?newOverride@ThingFactory@@AAE...`, a private member. It calls it directly, as
  Zero Hour's static member does. Under the `INI::` name, the TU needed an invented
  `friend class INI;` in ThingFactory to reach it.

The remaining access needs are consequences of the retail body, not of the name:

- The body writes INI::m_loadType inline (save, set 4, restore) for a ChildObject.
  The ini_noinline INI.h shim therefore declares `friend class ThingFactory;`.
- The body calls the protected ThingTemplate::validateAudio at 0x0013E2F0. The TU's
  local ThingTemplate therefore befriends ThingFactory.

The bytes are unchanged. The probe is EXACT at 541/541 for the body, and EXACT for
the three INIObject.cpp callers: 111/111 at 0x000C27E0, 151/151 at 0x000C2870 and
151/151 at 0x000C2930.
