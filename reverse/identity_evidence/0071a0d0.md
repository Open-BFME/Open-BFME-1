# The terrain's shroud (+0x30B8) and taint buffer (+0x30BC) are two classes

The pseudo-class `BaseHeightMapResetShroud` named both objects by offset. They are
W3DShroud and TaintBuffer.

* **Construction.** The terrain code stores the W3DShroud constructor's result
  (0x0071B590 through ILT 0x0004AFBB) into `[esi+0x30B8]` at 0x006CFEFB and the
  TaintBuffer constructor's result (0x00727530 through ILT 0x0003D77B) into
  `[esi+0x30BC]` at 0x006CFF36.
* **Receivers.** Every call through the ILT entries below loads `this` from the
  matching offset (+0x30B8 or +0x30BC; +0x2FF0 or +0x2FF4 in a second terrain
  layout), from the owning class's own methods, or from
  `W3DShaderManager::setShroudTex` for the texture getter.
* **No folding.** 0x0071A0D0 and 0x00727010 are byte-identical 14-byte bodies;
  retail has no identical-code folding, so they are two functions.

| Body | ILT | Identity | Previous name |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0x0071A0D0 | 0x00028420 | `W3DShroud::setBorderShroudLevel` (Zero Hour's body) | `setBorderShroudLevel30B8@BaseHeightMapResetShroud` |
| 0x0071A5E0 | 0x00045C46 | `W3DShroud::fillBorderShroudData` (pinned) | `fillShroudSurface@BaseHeightMapResetShroud` |
| 0x0071B4B0 | 0x0000AA4C | `W3DShroud::reset` (pinned) | `reset@Rva0071B4B0`, pin `reset30B8` |
| 0x0071B670 | 0x000029F5 | `W3DShroud::setShroudLevel` (row unchanged) | pin `setShroudLevel30B8` |
| 0x006D2630 | 0x00013BBF | `W3DShroud::getShroudTexture` (pinned) | `bfmeGet@Gen_006D2630` |
| 0x00727010 | 0x0000BFF5 | `TaintBuffer::setBorderShroudLevel` | `setBorderShroudLevel30BC@BaseHeightMapResetShroud` |
| 0x00727450 | 0x000272FF | `TaintBuffer::reset` | `reset30BC@BaseHeightMapResetShroud` |
| 0x00727610 | 0x0003D1DB | `TaintBuffer::setShroudLevel` | `setShroudLevel30BC@BaseHeightMapResetShroud` |

The TaintBuffer method names follow W3DShroud's, as the existing TaintBuffer rows
(`fill`, `init`, `collectDirtyCells`, `ReAcquireResources`) already do; the class
is proven by its constructor, the method names are analogies.

**Notification.** 0x006C8D10 is Zero Hour's
`BaseHeightMapRenderObjClass::notifyShroudChanged`, called from
W3DDisplay::setShroudLevel right after the level update, as in Zero Hour. It
notifies the child at +0x309C through ILT 0x00028B55 (`W3DPropBuffer::notifyShroudChanged`,
0x007024E0, as in Zero Hour) and, a BFME addition, the child at +0x3098 through
ILT 0x00005371 (0x0071CD20, still `BaseHeightMapResetBuffer::stampShroudVisible`;
its class is not established). The pins that named both children as
`BaseHeightMapResetShroud` now carry the names of the rows they reach.
