# `AIMoveToPositionAndDieState::onEnter` (0x0017EF50) and `AIMoveAndDeleteState::onEnter` (0x0017A430)

Two near-identical AI move-state onEnter bodies. The ledger had
`?onEnter@AIMoveAndDeleteState@@...` on 0x0017EF50; the vftables say it
belongs to the other class.

| body | table holding it (slot 4 = State::onEnter) | table slot 0 | slot 6 | CritterDesync label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0x0017A430 | 0x0109AAD8 | `??_GAIMoveAndDeleteState` | `?update@AIMoveAndDeleteState` (0x0017A530, matched) | "...setAdjustDestination(FALSE) 50" |
| 0x0017EF50 | 0x0109AB50 | `??_GAIMoveToPositionAndDieState` | `?update@AIMoveToPositionAndDieState` (0x0017F000, matched) | "...setAdjustDestination(FALSE) 60" |

Each body appears in exactly one table. Both are Zero Hour's
`AIMoveAndDeleteState::onEnter` shape (setAdjustsDestination(false), lock the
machine, copy the goal object's or goal position, set m_appendGoalPosition,
call AIInternalMoveToState::onEnter); 0x0017A430 also sets two
model-condition bits on the owner before the base call.
