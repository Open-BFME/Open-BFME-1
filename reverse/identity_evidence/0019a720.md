# Parser forwarder falsely claimed as weapon damage estimation

The 20-byte body at RVA0019A720 pushes its two incoming stack arguments and
a literal zero, retaining ECX, calls ILT0001366F and returns with ret8.
The ILT's five-byte jump independently resolves to RVA0019A470. That complete
543-byte body is matched as Rva0019A470Owner::parse(DataChunkInput&,
DataChunkInfo*, int) in Rva0019A470ChunkParser.cpp. It reads integer counts,
name keys, strings and coordinates from DataChunkInput, assembles BuildListInfo
records and appends them to the owner's selected list. Its successful exits
set AL=1 and pop12 bytes. It does not return floating-point weapon damage.
The wrapper passes list index zero and preserves that Boolean result.

The earlier Weapon::estimateWeaponDamage claim survived the independent
retirement of its false three-argument pin in ba415aca0d; that retirement
exposed this remaining dependent claim as unresolved in the full gate.
Restoring the Weapon pin would reintroduce a disproven identity. Instead the
wrapper uses the independently matched parser declaration and retains the
unknown original owner through the existing address-qualified parser owner.
No new pin or guessed game class is introduced. Original 20-byte extent is
preserved, with no net byte gain. The retired row remains in deleted_rows.csv.

A second Object::clearStatus(BitFlags) row claimed the same20 bytes through
AIPlayer.cpp. The same complete parser contract refutes that duplicate owner
and operation. It is separately tombstoned; the surplus identity count drops
by one while the covered address range remains unchanged.
